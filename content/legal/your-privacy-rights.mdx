### How to use them

Your pools and their candidates, activity log and history follow the rules [Privacy](/legal/privacy) sets out. "Delete my pools data" on your [pools settings page](/account) deletes your API key and every pool you own and takes you off the pools you edit, all at once. Your haruhime account (the osu! link and sessions) is deleted on [haruhime.moe/account](https://www.haruhime.moe/account).

Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile. Change them on osu!, and haruhime.moe copies the new ones the next time you sign in.

pools is small and free, and may fall below the thresholds where the CCPA applies. We give these rights to everyone anyway, wherever you live.
