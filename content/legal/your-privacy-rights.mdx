### How to use them

Your osu! account link, sessions, pools and their candidates, activity log and history follow the rules [Privacy](/legal/privacy) sets out. "Delete my account" on your account page deletes your account, every session, the link to osu!, every pool you own and takes you off the pools you edit, all at once.

Your name and avatar come from your osu! profile. Change them on osu!, and we copy the new ones the next time you sign in.

pools is small and free, and may fall below the thresholds where the CCPA applies. We give these rights to everyone anyway, wherever you live.
