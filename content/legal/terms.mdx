These are the rules for using pools.haruhime.moe. By signing in or making a pool you agree to them.

## The site

pools is a free, fan-made tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by ppy Pty Ltd or the osu! Tournament Committee. It comes as is: pages can be wrong or down, and we can change or stop any part of it.

## Your account

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, with osu!, on [haruhime.moe](https://www.haruhime.moe), the account every haruhime tool shares. Your account is your osu! account: we never see your osu! password, and your name and avatar come from osu!. [Privacy](/legal/privacy) says what we keep.

## Pools you make

You're responsible for what you put in a pool: its name, details, notes and maps. Public and unlisted pools can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in search. The editors you add can change a pool as you can, apart from who can see it, who edits it and deleting it. An unlisted or public pool with maps is also published as a pack on packs.haruhime.moe, crediting you and your editors, and packs' own rules and moderators apply to it there.

Don't use a pool for slurs, hate, harassment, spam, or to pass yourself off as someone else. Names, details and notes go through a content filter, but it only catches some of that.

## Moderation

Admins can hide or delete any pool that breaks these rules, and remove an account that keeps breaking them.

## Deleting

You can delete a pool, or everything you keep on pools with "Delete my pools data" on your pools settings page, at any time. Your haruhime account itself is deleted on haruhime.moe/account. Deleting happens at once and can't be undone.
