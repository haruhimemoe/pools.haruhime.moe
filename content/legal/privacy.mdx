This page says what pools.haruhime.moe stores, why, and for how long.

## If you visit

We set no cookies unless you sign in, and run no analytics.

To stop floods, we count search and check requests per IP address (an IPv6 address by its /64) in counters that are deleted automatically within about two minutes. The check also counts, the same way, how many osu! API calls each IP address caused.

Text and filters you type into the search of every osu! map are sent to the hinai mirror (mirror.hinamizawa.ai) to run the search.

The check keeps what the osu! API says about each beatmapset it looked up (status, artist, title, source, tags, Featured Artist track and download availability) for 24 hours, so the next check is faster. That's about the maps, not about you.

## If you sign in

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, to make pools. For you we store the osu! user ID, username, avatar URL and country osu! sends, a record linking the account to osu! (never osu! tokens), session records that keep you signed in, with the IP address and browser User-Agent each session started from, and the pools you make.

When you're signed in, we also count, per account, how many pool changes, new pools and editor changes you make, in counters that are deleted automatically within about two hours.

Signing in sets a session cookie, a short-lived one while sign-in runs, and `pools-signed-in`, which pages can read. It only says "this browser may be signed in", so pages ask who you are only when it's there. Sessions last 7 days, and expired ones are deleted automatically.

## Pools you make

A pool keeps its name, details, notes and maps, who owns it, the editors the owner adds, and when it changed. To add an editor, the owner types their osu! username and we look it up on the osu! API; we keep their osu! ID and username, even before they ever sign in. Public and unlisted pools can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in search. Private pools are seen only by you and the editors you add. Admins can hide or delete any pool that breaks the [terms](/legal/terms).

## Deleting

"Delete my account" on your account page deletes your account, every session, the link to osu!, every pool you own (and its pack on packs.haruhime.moe) at once, and takes you off the pools you edit. Deleting a pool removes it for good.

## What we never do

We don't sell or share any of this, and we don't host beatmap files.
