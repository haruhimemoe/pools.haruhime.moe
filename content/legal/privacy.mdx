This page says what pools.haruhime.moe stores, why, and for how long.

## If you visit

We set no cookies unless you sign in, and run no analytics.

To stop floods, we count search and check requests per IP address (an IPv6 address by its /64) in counters that are deleted automatically within about two minutes. The check also counts, the same way, how many osu! API calls each IP address caused.

Text and filters you type into the search of every osu! map are sent to the hinai mirror (mirror.hinamizawa.ai) to run the search.

Map covers and preview clips load in your browser straight from osu!'s servers (assets.ppy.sh and b.ppy.sh), as avatars do from a.ppy.sh, so osu! sees your IP address when they load. A clip plays only when you press its button. We never pass these files through our server.

The check keeps what the osu! API says about each beatmapset it looked up (status, artist, title, source, tags, Featured Artist track and download availability) for 24 hours, so the next check is faster. That's about the maps, not about you.

## If you sign in

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, to make pools. For you we store the osu! user ID, username, avatar URL and country osu! sends, a record linking the account to osu! (never osu! tokens), session records that keep you signed in, with the IP address and browser User-Agent each session started from, and the pools you make.

When you're signed in, we also count, per account, how many pool changes, new pools, editor changes, pack updates and account deletions you make, in counters that are deleted automatically within about two hours. These counters use your osu! user ID, so deleting your account and signing in again doesn't reset them.

Signing in sets a session cookie, a short-lived one while sign-in runs, and `pools-signed-in`, which pages can read. It only says "this browser may be signed in", so pages ask who you are only when it's there. A session lasts until 7 days after you last use the site, and expired ones are deleted automatically.

## Pools you make

A pool keeps its name, details, notes and maps, who owns it, the editors the owner adds, and when it changed. To add an editor, the owner types their osu! username and we look it up on the osu! API; we keep their osu! ID and username, even before they ever sign in. Public and unlisted pools can be seen by anyone with the link, and public ones are listed in search. A shared pool's page shows the osu! usernames and IDs of its owner and editors. Private pools are seen only by you and the editors you add. Admins can hide or delete any pool that breaks the [terms](/legal/terms).

Each change to a pool goes in its activity log: who made it (osu! ID and username), when, and what changed. Only the pool's owner and editors see it. A pool keeps its last 200 changes, each for at most 180 days. Deleting the pool deletes its log; deleting your account replaces your name in other pools' logs with "deleted user" and removes your osu! ID from them.

Unlisted and public pools with maps also get a pack on packs.haruhime.moe, for downloads: we send packs the pool's name, its maps, and a line crediting its owner and editors by osu! username with a link back here, again after each change. packs lists the pack only while the pool is public and not hidden. Making a pool private or deleting it removes its pack there.

## Deleting

"Delete my account" on your account page deletes your account, every session, the link to osu!, every pool you own (and its pack on packs.haruhime.moe) at once, and takes you off the pools you edit. Deleting a pool removes it for good. If packs.haruhime.moe doesn't answer, your account and pools are still deleted, and we keep only each pool's ID and why the removal failed until its pack is gone there, trying again from time to time.

## What we never do

We don't sell or share any of this, and we don't host beatmap files.
