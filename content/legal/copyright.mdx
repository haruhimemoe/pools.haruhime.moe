Past pools come from the public export of [otdb](https://otdb.sheppsu.me), by Sheppsu, used with his permission, or from tournament hosts and community members, whom [Disclaimers](/legal/disclaimers) says more about. A pool page names its sources.

## Beatmap files

pools never hosts beatmap files. The all-maps search and a pool's star ratings, AR, OD, BPM and length come from the hinai mirror (mirror.hinamizawa.ai), which [Disclaimers](/legal/disclaimers) says more about. To report a beatmap itself, write to the mirror or to ppy Pty Ltd, not us.
